Returns & Refunds

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Because candy and food products are perishable, returns are generally not accepted after delivery.

Damaged or incorrect order

If an order arrives damaged or an incorrect product is delivered, contact Candyaura as soon as possible with the order details and, where useful, a photo of the issue. We will review the case and may offer a replacement or another appropriate resolution.

Order cancellation

Cancellation requests should be made before the order is prepared or dispatched. Once an order has been prepared or dispatched, cancellation may not be possible.
